## Rationale

The draft smooths over conflicting source information. Source note A describes lower left tooth-area discomfort that is most noticeable when chewing. Source note B describes symptoms as improving and "not clearly in one spot." The timing and relationship between the two notes are unclear.

The packet should preserve the conflict instead of presenting a confident summary that the situation is resolving or that no distinct concern is apparent.

## Required Revisions

- Preserve what each source note says.
- State that timing between the notes is unclear.
- Remove unsupported certainty about a single improving course.
- Avoid implying the packet resolves the source conflict.
- Keep the output framed as a non-diagnostic healthcare-adjacent review artifact.
