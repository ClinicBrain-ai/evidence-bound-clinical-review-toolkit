# Review Rules

1. Treat AI-generated healthcare-adjacent output as unverified draft text.
2. Link material claims to supplied source evidence when possible.
3. Flag missing information that limits responsible review.
4. Mark unsupported claims explicitly.
5. Escalate outputs that imply diagnosis, treatment, medication guidance, urgency, or patient-specific advice.
6. Require human review before any downstream use.
7. Record reviewer decisions and audit events.
8. Do not use this toolkit with real patient data.
9. Do not represent the packet as clinical validation.
10. Do not use the packet as a medical device output or clinical decision.
