# Before / After

## Before

AI produced a fluent healthcare-adjacent summary, but reviewers could not easily verify what it relied on.

The draft could sound complete while leaving important review questions unanswered:

- Which source details supported each claim?
- Which claims were unsupported?
- What information was missing?
- Did the output drift beyond the available facts?
- Should a human reviewer approve, revise, reject, or escalate it?

## After

The same output is converted into a Clinical Review Packet with evidence links, missing-information flags, risk level, human review status, reviewer decision, and audit events.

The packet does not make the output clinically correct. It makes the output easier to review by exposing:

- available evidence
- missing inputs
- risk routing
- review requirement
- reviewer decision
- audit trail

The goal is reviewability, not autonomous clinical judgment.
